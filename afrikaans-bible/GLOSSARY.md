# GLOSSARY.md — Committed Terminology

> Every term in this glossary has a committed Afrikaans rendering. Claude must
> follow these renderings unless explicitly overruled in a chapter's notes
> (with reason). New terms are added during translation work and reviewed by
> the team before being committed here.
>
> **Format**: `Source (transliteration) → Afrikaans rendering | notes`
>
> **Status legend**:
> - ✅ Committed — use this rendering, no exceptions
> - 🟡 Provisional — used pending team review
> - 🔴 Open — multiple candidates, decision needed before next chapter

## Proper names policy

- **People and places**: use established Afrikaans forms (Moses, Abraham, Jerusalem, Egipte, Babilon).
- **Lesser-known names**: transliterate from Hebrew/Greek using standard Afrikaans phonology.
- **When uncertain**: check AFR53 for the *form* only (this is a fact about Afrikaans usage, not copyrighted text). Do not lift surrounding context.
